# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- The mod now applies only after the Great Schism by default: from the start in games that begin after it (1066 and 1178 bookmarks), and from the moment it happens in earlier games. Before the Schism, and if it is prevented, Ecumenical Councils work as in vanilla.
- New game rule **HFCV: Head of Faith Council Veto** (After the Great Schism / Always) to apply the mod for the whole game instead.
- Fixed a line break in the Steam Workshop link of the README.

## 1.0.0

- First release.
- At Ecumenical Councils hosted by an AI who is not the Head of Faith:
  - An AI Head of Faith is added to the council when it is called, and the council is cancelled when it begins if the Head of Faith never joined it.
  - An AI Head of Faith always opposes: they start as a Conservative, are never moved to the Innovators and cannot be swayed.
  - The Head of Faith's opposition is a veto: a proposal they oppose fails even with a majority, and the result explains it. An absent Head of Faith cannot approve anything either.
- Councils hosted by a player, a player's theological puppet or the Head of Faith work as in vanilla.
- English and Spanish localization; other languages fall back to English.
